THE BEGINNING

Where It Started

It all started on the way to tuition at Ahmadhiyya School. At the time, it seemed like just another ordinary day, but then Ainy asked for my number. Neither of us knew that one little moment would lead to us talking every day and eventually becoming us.

The first conversation

Everday

Ainy asked for my number, and soon we started talking every day. What started with simple conversations slowly became something we both looked forward to, and those everyday messages became a big part of our story.

Our first date

Mr. sub

Our very first actual date was at Mr. Sub. It meant even more because both of our parents knew about us and knew we were going on our first date together. It was the first time our story felt like something real beyond our everyday conversations.

1.6 ANNIVERSARY

Butter Chicken & Spider-Man

For one of our recent anniversaries, we went out for butter chicken and then watched Spider-Man together. It wasn't about doing anything extravagant. It was simply having good food, watching a movie, and spending another special day together. And seeing you happy made my entire day even better.
